[hz_needle] Needle-stick injury to a clinician, or needle misinsertion into the patient, during contrast administration.
  - needle_gauge_range: The system shall support single-use needles with gauge sizes from 18G to 24G for peripheral IV access.
      - needle_gauge_lockout: The injector shall reject a programmed flow rate that exceeds the maximum rate rated for the detected needle gauge.
  - needle_shield_engagement: The system shall provide a passive needle-shielding mechanism that engages automatically upon withdrawal.
      - needle_shield_selftest: The needle-shielding mechanism shall present a visible indicator confirming it has engaged before the needle is discarded.
  - needle_compat_labeling: The system shall display the compatible needle gauge range on the injector head housing.
      - needle_label_multilang: The compatible needle gauge label shall be provided in English and at least one additional facility-configurable language.

[hz_catheter] Leakage or disconnection at the syringe-to-catheter connection during injection, causing contrast extravasation or line disconnection under pressure.
  - luer_lock_torque: The syringe-to-catheter connector shall be a standard luer-lock fitting rated to withstand injection pressures up to 325 psi without disconnection.
      - luer_lock_indicator: The system shall provide a visible or tactile confirmation that the luer-lock connection is fully seated before permitting injection.
  - line_leak_detection: The system shall detect a pressure drop consistent with a line disconnect or leak within 1 second of onset (revised from 2 seconds after a design review) and halt injection.
      - leak_alarm_latency: The leak-detection alarm shall annunciate within 500 ms of leak detection.
      - line_leak_selftest: The pressure sensor used for leak detection shall execute a zero-offset self-check before each injection.
  - catheter_compat_matrix: The system shall restrict programmable flow rate and pressure limits to values compatible with the catheter gauge selected by the operator.
      - catheter_compat_override: An operator override of the catheter compatibility restriction shall require a two-step confirmation and shall be logged.

[hz_flow] Delivery of contrast at an incorrect flow rate or volume, producing a clinically inadequate or unsafe bolus.
  - flow_rate_accuracy: The injector shall deliver programmed flow rate within +/-3% of setpoint (revised from +/-5% after a design review) across the rated range of 0.1 to 10 mL/s.
      - flow_rate_closed_loop: The pump control loop shall sample the flow sensor at 100 Hz and adjust motor drive within one control cycle.
      - flow_rate_calibration: The injector shall support a field flow-rate calibration procedure traceable to a certified reference volume.
  - volume_accuracy: The injector shall deliver the programmed total volume within +/-2% for volumes between 5 and 200 mL.
      - volume_accuracy_calibration: The system shall support a field volume-accuracy verification procedure using a calibrated graduated cylinder.
  - dual_syringe_sync: For dual-syringe (contrast/saline) configurations, the system shall synchronize switchover to within 50 ms of the programmed transition point.
      - dual_syringe_ratio: The system shall support programmable contrast:saline mixing ratios from 100:0 to 0:100 in 10% increments.
  - max_pressure_limit: The injector shall enforce an operator-programmable maximum line pressure limit between 50 and 325 psi.
      - max_pressure_derate: The enforced maximum pressure limit shall automatically derate by 20% when ambient temperature exceeds 30 C, per the pump's thermal rating.

[hz_air] Injection of air into the patient's vasculature (air embolism).
  - air_sensor_detection: The system shall detect an air column of 0.05 mL or greater (revised from 0.10 mL after a design review) in the fluid path using ultrasonic air-in-line sensing.
      - air_sensor_response: Upon air detection, the system shall halt the pump motor within 100 ms.
      - air_sensor_selftest: The air-in-line sensor shall execute a power-on self-test and fault to a safe state if the self-test fails.
  - air_purge_procedure: The system shall provide a guided air-purge priming sequence before the line is connected to the patient.
      - air_purge_verification: Completion of the air-purge sequence shall be verified by the air-in-line sensor reading clear before the system enables patient connection.
  - air_alarm_annunciation: The system shall annunciate a distinct audible and visual alarm pattern specific to air-in-line detection, distinguishable from occlusion alarms.

[hz_reservoir] Administration of expired, mismatched, or depleted contrast media due to untracked reservoir inventory or identity.
  - reservoir_level_sensing: The system shall continuously monitor contrast reservoir fill level and estimate remaining deliverable volume within +/-3 mL.
      - low_reservoir_warning: The system shall issue a low-reservoir warning when remaining volume falls below the volume required to complete the programmed protocol.
  - lot_expiration_tracking: The system shall record the contrast lot number and expiration date scanned at syringe load and block injection past the expiration date.
      - lot_expiration_grace: The system shall warn, but not block, injection of contrast within 24 hours of its expiration date, and shall block outright after expiration.
  - reservoir_barcode_scan: The system shall verify contrast concentration and volume against a scanned barcode before enabling injection.
      - reservoir_barcode_mismatch: A barcode scan that does not match the previously loaded lot shall require operator re-confirmation before proceeding.

[hz_occlusion] Patient injury from a line occlusion or extravasation that goes undetected before injury occurs.
  - occlusion_pressure_threshold: The system shall detect a line occlusion when measured pressure exceeds the operator-set occlusion threshold for more than 300 ms.
      - occlusion_threshold_range: The occlusion pressure threshold shall be programmable from 10 to 300 psi in 5 psi increments.
      - occlusion_response_halt: Upon occlusion detection, the pump shall halt motor drive within 200 ms and hold the last commanded position.
  - extravasation_pattern_detect: The system shall flag a suspected extravasation pattern when pressure rises gradually without reaching the hard occlusion threshold, using a slope-based heuristic.
      - extravasation_operator_alert: A suspected extravasation pattern shall present a distinct on-screen advisory recommending clinical assessment, separate from the hard occlusion alarm.
  - occlusion_alarm_priority: Occlusion alarms shall be assigned the highest annunciation priority level in the alarm system, per IEC 60601-1-8 style prioritization.

[hz_alarm] A clinically significant alarm condition that goes unnoticed, is silenced too long, or is confused with a different alarm.
  - alarm_priority_levels: The system shall implement three alarm priority levels (high, medium, low) each with a distinct audible pattern per IEC 60601-1-8.
      - alarm_visual_coding: Each alarm priority level shall be paired with a distinct visual indicator color (red, yellow, cyan).
  - alarm_silence_timeout: An operator-initiated alarm silence shall time out and re-annunciate after no more than 120 seconds if the underlying condition persists.
      - alarm_silence_max_duration: The maximum single silence duration for a high-priority alarm shall not exceed 2 minutes, per IEC 60601-1-8.
  - alarm_log_retention: The system shall retain the most recent 500 alarm events in non-volatile storage with timestamp and acknowledgment record.
      - alarm_log_export: The system shall support exporting the alarm log in a structured, human-readable format for service review.

[hz_ui] Entry of a clinically unsafe injection parameter that the system accepts and executes.
  - param_range_validation: The user interface shall reject entry of a flow rate, volume, or pressure limit outside the clinically validated range for the selected protocol.
      - param_confirm_dialog: The user interface shall require a second, explicit confirmation before accepting a programmed parameter that exceeds a soft warning threshold.
  - protocol_library: The system shall provide a library of at least 10 preconfigured injection protocols selectable by exam type.
      - protocol_library_custom: The system shall allow a site administrator to add up to 20 custom protocols to the library, subject to the same range validation as built-in protocols.
  - ui_lockout_cleaning: The user interface shall lock out parameter entry while the system reports an active cleaning or reprocessing cycle.
      - ui_lockout_override: A supervisor override of the cleaning-cycle lockout shall require a service PIN and shall be logged.

[hz_power] Unsafe pump behavior, or loss of in-progress protocol state, during a mains power interruption.
  - battery_backup_runtime: The system shall provide battery backup capable of completing an in-progress injection and safely parking the pump for at least 5 minutes after loss of mains power.
      - battery_health_monitor: The system shall monitor battery state of health and issue a service alert when capacity falls below 80% of rated.
  - power_loss_state_preserve: Upon mains power loss, the system shall preserve the in-progress protocol state and resume without requiring re-programming once power is restored.
  - ground_fault_protection: The system shall detect a ground fault condition on the patient-connected circuit and disconnect within the time limits of IEC 60601-1.
      - ground_fault_selftest: Ground fault protection circuitry shall be exercised by a self-test at power-on.

[hz_data] An incomplete or tamper-undetectable injection record, compromising the patient record or a later audit.
  - injection_record_completeness: The system shall record programmed and actually-delivered flow rate, volume, and pressure profile for every injection.
      - injection_record_integrity: Each injection record shall include a checksum to detect post-hoc tampering.
      - injection_record_export: The system shall support exporting injection records in a structured format for integration with the hospital information system.
  - audit_trail_user_actions: The system shall log operator identity and timestamp for every parameter change, in a separate append-only audit trail.
      - audit_trail_retention: The audit trail shall retain at least 2 years of operator action records before the oldest records may be archived.
  - clock_sync_ntp: The system shall synchronize its internal clock to facility network time within +/-1 second when connected to the network.
      - clock_sync_fallback: If network time is unavailable, the system shall fall back to its internal real-time clock and flag records with a reduced-confidence timestamp source.

[hz_sterilization] Cross-contamination between patients from a reusable component that was reused without validated, confirmed reprocessing.
  - reprocessing_cycle_validation: Reusable fluid-path components shall be validated to withstand the facility's specified high-level disinfection cycle for a minimum of 100 cycles.
      - reprocessing_cycle_count_display: The system shall display the remaining validated reprocessing cycle count for a reusable component that reports a use-counter.
  - single_use_component_lockout: The system shall detect an already-used single-use syringe or tubing set, via an embedded use-counter chip, and prevent reuse.
      - single_use_lockout_override: An override of the single-use lockout shall require a documented service-level authorization code, logged in the audit trail.
  - housing_material_disinfectant: External housing materials shall be compatible with the facility's list of approved surface disinfectants without degradation over 500 wipe cycles.

[hz_mechanical] Loss of basic safety function from a mechanical or environmental event (drop, tip-over, fluid ingress) in the imaging suite.
  - ip_rating_fluid_ingress: The injector head shall meet at least IP34 fluid-ingress protection to tolerate incidental contrast spillage.
  - emc_immunity: The system shall meet IEC 60601-1-2 electromagnetic immunity requirements for use adjacent to CT and angiography equipment.
      - emc_mri_conditional: If offered in an MRI-conditional configuration, the system shall be labeled per ASTM F2503 MRI conditional labeling requirements.
  - drop_shock_rating: The mobile console shall withstand a 75 cm drop onto a hard floor on any face without loss of basic safety function.
  - caster_brake_stability: The mobile console shall not tip over when the loaded injector head arm is extended to its maximum reach at any caster-locked orientation.
      - caster_lock_indicator: The system shall provide a visible indicator of caster-lock engagement state.

[hz_swsafety] A runaway or hung main control loop that reaches the patient before an independent safety monitor can intervene.
  - watchdog_timer_reset: An independent hardware watchdog timer shall force a safe-state stop if the main control loop fails to service it within 250 ms.
      - watchdog_selftest_poweron: The watchdog circuit shall be exercised as part of power-on self-test before the system is placed in a ready state.
  - dual_channel_pressure_check: Line pressure shall be independently measured by two physically separate sensors, and injection shall halt if the two readings disagree by more than 15%.
  - software_version_integrity: The system shall verify a cryptographic signature on the control software image at boot and refuse to run an unsigned or modified image.
      - sw_update_rollback: A failed software update shall automatically roll back to the last known-good verified image.
  - safe_state_definition: Following any detected fault, the system shall enter a defined safe state, motor de-energized, valve closed, alarm active, within 500 ms.

[hz_network] A network fault or EMR data mismatch that blocks a needed injection, or lets one proceed against unvalidated worklist data.
  - network_isolation_degrade: Loss of network connectivity to the EMR/RIS shall not prevent the system from delivering a locally-programmed injection.
  - emr_worklist_integrity: Patient worklist data received from the EMR shall be validated, patient ID and exam type, against the operator's manual entry before an injection is enabled.
      - emr_data_encryption: Data exchanged with the EMR/RIS over the network shall be encrypted in transit using TLS 1.2 or higher.
  - remote_service_access_auth: Remote service access to the injector's diagnostic interface shall require multi-factor authentication and shall be logged.
      - remote_access_session_timeout: A remote service session shall automatically terminate after 15 minutes of inactivity.
